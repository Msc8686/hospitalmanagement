ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Smart Hospital Management

Making Hospital Management Easier

The Hospital Appointment and Patient Management System is designed to digitize important hospital activities.

Patients can register, check doctors, book appointments, reschedule or cancel appointments and view their medical records.

Doctors can view appointments and patient information and manage medical records.

Administrators can manage patients, doctors and appointments and generate hospital reports.

💙

Efficient & Organized

Reduce paperwork and make hospital operations easier to manage.
